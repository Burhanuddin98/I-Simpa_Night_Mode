## What is proven, and what is not

**Proven.** The GUI builds from a clean checkout, 61 of 61 targets. The four solvers
build from the pinned tag and run. The test suite links and runs again, 17 of 19, both
failures environmental rather than code — one hardcodes a path to Zeph, which is its own
defect worth fixing. On the corrected hall the whole chain runs end to end: TetGen skips
nothing, all 7,860 tet faces match, 24,081 tetrahedra, SPPS reports no particle error,
and the GUI renders the result with particle trails playing.

**Not proven, and this is the blocker.** Nothing about accuracy. Arc item 12: on the hall
as upstream ships it, SPPS loses 599,982 of 600,000 particles and still exits 0, because
the geometry self-intersects, TetGen refuses it with exit 3, the pipeline downgrades that
to a warning, and the solver's complaint goes to stderr which the GUI never reads. Four
silent failures in a row. **Every acoustic number measured before that discovery is
retracted** and must not be quoted from any earlier note.
