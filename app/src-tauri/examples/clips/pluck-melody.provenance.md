# pluck-melody.wav
- Title: A plucked-string melody (generated by this repository's code, not recorded)
- Made by: tools/clips/make_dry_clips.py (`python tools/clips/make_dry_clips.py`; `--check` regenerates and compares byte for byte), seed 0x4e4d44525931 through numpy's PCG64, numpy 2.5.2; the three clips are drawn from one generator in the order clap-pattern, pluck-melody, drum-groove
- Licence: GPL-3.0-only, as the app (the script and what it writes are this repository's own work)
- Source page: none; synthesised, nothing was recorded or fetched
- sha256: 8456453fd32a4a486158e586a713c7339a5246eb5cdbe1eb0f2a015adaa9900e
- Format: 16.875 s, 48 kHz, mono, 16-bit PCM, peak-normalised to -1.0 dBFS (gain 0.920366 on the synthesised samples), no dither
- What it is: 26 notes (19 staccato, 7 held), MIDI 45 to 72 (A2 to C5), an original tune in A minor at 96 beats a minute with rests between its four phrases. Each note is a modal plucked string: partials at k f0 sqrt(1 + 1e-4 k^2) up to 12 kHz, amplitudes |sin(k pi p)|/k^1.3 for a pluck position p drawn in 0.14-0.22, each partial decaying with a T60 of 4 s (220 Hz/f)^0.75 (at most 5 s), a 0.4 ms attack and a 3 ms pluck noise. A staccato note sounds 0.14 s, a held note its length less 0.12 s; then the string is damped (the release). Levels random per note within 1.5 dB, staccato 2 dB below held.
- Each event's end (its release): damped by 60 dB in 35 ms (exponentially) and faded to exact zero by 40 ms (raised cosine).
- Dryness: anechoic by construction (no room, no microphone, no reverberation in the synthesis). Measured on this file as written (read back): for each of its 26 events, the time from its end until every 1 ms frame up to the next event is 60 dB or more below the mean square of the millisecond before the end: at most 38 ms, median 35 ms (criterion: within 50 ms). After every event the file is digital zero until the next onset: those silences last 0.082 to 1.028 s.
