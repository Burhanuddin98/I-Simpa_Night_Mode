# drum-groove.wav
- Title: A drum groove (kick, snare, hi-hat) (generated by this repository's code, not recorded)
- Made by: tools/clips/make_dry_clips.py (`python tools/clips/make_dry_clips.py`; `--check` regenerates and compares byte for byte), seed 0x4e4d44525931 through numpy's PCG64, numpy 2.5.2; the three clips are drawn from one generator in the order clap-pattern, pluck-melody, drum-groove
- Licence: GPL-3.0-only, as the app (the script and what it writes are this repository's own work)
- Source page: none; synthesised, nothing was recorded or fetched
- sha256: 065484635f6e059a927f9dadcb87e167fdb3a2fe577b66ffc088903a3dbc4ad9
- Format: 10.150 s, 48 kHz, mono, 16-bit PCM, peak-normalised to -1.0 dBFS (gain 0.354684 on the synthesised samples), no dither
- What it is: 25 hits at 100 beats a minute on an eighth-note grid: two bars, a 1.2 s break, a third bar and a last kick and snare together, then 1.5 s of silence. Kick: a sine falling from 140 to 50 Hz (time constant 30 ms) decaying with 120 ms, and a 1.5 ms click, 180 ms before its release. Snare: 185 and 330 Hz tones (30 ms) and noise band-passed 1-8 kHz (50 ms), 150 ms. Closed hi-hat: noise band-passed 6-16 kHz (12 ms), 50 ms, 10 dB below the kick. Hits sharing an onset are one event, released when its longest part is.
- Each event's end (its release): damped by 60 dB in 35 ms (exponentially) and faded to exact zero by 40 ms (raised cosine).
- Dryness: anechoic by construction (no room, no microphone, no reverberation in the synthesis). Measured on this file as written (read back): for each of its 25 events, the time from its end until every 1 ms frame up to the next event is 60 dB or more below the mean square of the millisecond before the end: at most 34 ms, median 32 ms (criterion: within 50 ms). After every event the file is digital zero until the next onset: those silences last 0.085 to 1.317 s.
