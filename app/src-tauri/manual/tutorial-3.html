Tutorial 3: an industrial hall

Upstream I-Simpa's third tutorial, done in Night Mode. The manual · Tutorial 1 · Tutorial 2

This is upstream I-Simpa's tutorial Study of an industrial room (Docs/tutorial_industrial_hall.rst): a factory hall coupled through a door to a corridor and another room, two milling machines of three sources each, two fitting zones, walls that transmit sound, and at the end the effect of one absorbing wall. The project is upstream's tutorial_3.proj, set as the text says where the two differ.

Where upstream's project and its text differ, the text is followed: the project has white noise, the zones at alpha 0.2 and 0 with a mean free path of 1 m, Absorbing_material on door_room2, the door closed at 125 Hz, 50 000 particles over 2 s and one band. The receivers are the project's, on the line (k − 1, k, 1.6); the text's grid table describes another line. Receiver 1 is moved 0.5 m along that line, off the wall it touched in the project: Night Mode refuses a receiver on a surface.

In Night Mode

  1. Help › Tutorial 3: an industrial hall, or its card on the start page, opens your own copy.
  2. Simulate: run SPPS (rst:375). This is the reference.
  3. Materials: pick the group diff_wall and choose Absorbing_material (rst:433). Save (Ctrl+S).
  4. Simulate: run SPPS again with the same settings (rst:435).
  5. Results: with the second run selected, switch on Difference from baseline in the Map card. The baseline is the run before it, the reference, so the map is the second run minus the first, in dB: upstream's Compare surface receivers (rst:437-445).

Upstream builds the scene with tools this version does without: copying and moving a group of sources (here the machines are already placed), the receiver grid and the comparison tool's own result file.

What upstream expects

A picture: the difference map (rst:446-450, Screenshot_6_tutorial_3.PNG), all bands, cumulated. Its legend runs from about −0.1 dB to about −1.8 dB, with contours at −0.5 dB and −1.0 dB: the absorbing wall lowers the level everywhere on the plane, least around the machines, most at the end of the hall by the origin. The text prints no value; these are read off the figure's legend.

On this build

  • The difference: reproduced. Both runs end OK with nothing on the solver's error output, and their results pass the app's check. Cumulated over the run, the second minus the first spans the plane over the range upstream's legend shows, within about a tenth of a decibel at either end, and falls the same way: least around the machines, most at the end of the hall by the origin.
  • Not drawn as upstream draws it. The Map card draws the difference step by step, averaged over a short time window; it does not cumulate a difference, so the figure's cumulated map is not on screen in this version. The comparison above was made from the two runs' own files.
  • Not a variant. diff_wall shares its material's solver id with the other groups on 30 % absorbing, as upstream's project numbers them, and a variant that changes one group of such a set is refused when the run is prepared. Changing the group's own material, as upstream's text does, runs.

The comparison, its method and the numbers are recorded with the app's source (docs/investigations/2026-10-09-tutorials/README.md); the two gaps are rows 109 and 110 of docs/v1.1-backlog.md.
