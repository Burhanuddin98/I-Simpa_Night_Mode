This is upstream I-Simpa's tutorial Study of the Elmia hall (Docs/tutorial_Elmia_hall.rst), the concert hall of the second Round Robin on room-acoustics simulation, on the project upstream ships with it, tutorial_2.proj, with SPPS set as the text's calculation step says.

Where the project and the text differ, the project is kept: R02 is at (12.9, 10.2, 5.3) in the project and (12.9, 10.5, 5.3) in the text (rst:108).

The geometry steps are not in this version. Upstream imports the raw elmia.ply, whose surfaces intersect (the text says so, rst:51), and rebuilds it with Average model remesh (rst:53-96). Night Mode has no average-model remesh; the project carries the hall upstream's own correction left, which meshes as it is.

In Night Mode

  1. Help › Tutorial 2: the Elmia hall, or its card on the start page, opens your own copy.
  2. Simulate: SPPS is set as the text says. Run it (F5, rst:161).
  3. Results: the Acoustics tab gives the parameters at R01 to R06. Values that ISO 3382-1 defines per source and receiver (EDT, T20, T30, C50, C80, D50, Ts) are given for one source at a time: choose it under Source. Open response shows R01's energy response, as upstream's echogram does; the Map card draws the two planes.

What upstream expects

  • The echogram at R01 (rst:175-179, the figure tutorial_2_Results_Echogram.PNG, computed with 1 000 000 particles per source): all bands and all three sources together, with its Schroeder curve. The text prints no value; read off the figure, the Schroeder curve falls about 33 dB per second between 0.2 s and 1.0 s, a decay of about 1.8 s for 60 dB.
  • The noise maps of the two planes (rst:181-185), a picture with no scale.
  • The volume of the remeshed hall, 9624 m³ (rst:80).

On this build

  • The echogram at R01: reproduced. Run as shipped (100 000 particles per source), R01's energy summed over the bands and the sources, read the same way as upstream's figure, decays at the rate the figure shows, within what can be read off a picture.
  • The volume: not reproduced, and not comparable. The hall here is the corrected geometry upstream's project carries, not a new average-model remesh, so its air volume (on the status bar) is not the 9624 m³ the remesh gives.
  • The maps have no scale in upstream's figure, so there is nothing to compare but their look.

The comparison, its method and the numbers are recorded with the app's source (docs/investigations/2026-10-09-tutorials/README.md).
