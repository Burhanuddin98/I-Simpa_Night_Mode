Written 2026-09-25 05:40, before any calibration run.

WHAT IS MEASURED
- A cell: one room, walls, method, particle count, duration, time step, trans_epsilon; octave bands
  125 Hz to 4 kHz; 6 point receivers; SPPS seeds 1 to 10. The bands and receivers of a cell are its
  receiver-bands (36).
- Per receiver-band and quantity (SPL, EDT, T20, T30, C50, C80, D50, Ts): the value from each seed's
  series as the bootstrap evaluates its resamples (complete, early reverberation unresolved as the
  report reads it, measured from the report's arrival). Only receiver-bands where every seed gives a
  value are used (no seed selected away).
- OBSERVED: the sample standard deviation of the value over the 10 seeds (relative to the mean for
  EDT, T20, T30).
- PREDICTED: the root-mean-square over the seeds of the model's standard deviation for that seed's
  series (relative to that seed's value for the decay times).
- A cell's POOLED RATIO per quantity: sqrt(sum observed^2 / sum predicted^2) over its receiver-bands,
  with dof = receiver-bands x (seeds - 1). Its one-sided 95 % bounds: ratio * sqrt(dof / chi2(dof, q))
  with q = 0.05 (upper bound) and 0.95 (lower bound).

RULES, fixed now
1. Structure. Random mode keeps M7's structure (compound Poisson crossings of constant mean deposit;
   it is exact in structure there). Energetic mode: the candidates are (a) M7's constant deposit and
   (b) a per-bin deposit from the particles' mean energy (the room table), and any further candidate
   is added before the validation cells are looked at. The candidate is chosen on the CALIBRATION
   cells only: the one whose calibrated model overstates least (geometric mean over calibration cells
   and quantities of k / pooled ratio).
2. Calibration factor per mode and quantity: k = the largest, over the calibration cells of that
   mode, of the one-sided 95 % UPPER bound of the pooled ratio. The calibrated standard deviation is
   k times the model's. Rounded UP to 2 significant digits. Never below the observed on the
   calibration cells by construction.
3. Validation, run once after k is fixed and committed: on every validation cell and quantity, the
   one-sided 95 % LOWER bound of (observed / calibrated prediction) must be at most 1 ("the
   prediction is at or above the observed within its statistical uncertainty"). A failure is
   reported as a failure; k is not re-fitted on validation cells.
4. 1/sqrt(N): for the cell pairs that differ only in N (C-R1/C-R2, C-R3/C-R4, C-E1/C-E2,
   C-E3/C-E4, C-R5/V-R4 and V-E4 against C-E5 where the floor allows), observed * sqrt(N) must
   agree within their joint uncertainty (2 standard errors) for the particle count a refusal names
   to be taken from the 1/sqrt(N) scaling. Where it does not, that is reported and the refusal
   names no count for that mode or quantity.
5. Say-NO partners, in the normal suite on the committed numbers: the factors halved fail the
   validation; one quantity's observed noise raised above its prediction fails that quantity's check.
6. Refusal limits are unchanged (Burhan's decisions): EDT, T20, T30 2.5 % relative; C50, C80 0.5 dB;
   D50 0.025; Ts 5 ms; SPL 0.5 dB.

THE MODEL (fixed now; its numbers are fixed by the rules below on the calibration cells): the
calibrated standard deviation of a quantity is k * sqrt(1 + kappa * n) * sd, sd the bootstrap's
(params::noise::bootstrap, unchanged), n the run's multi-crossing variable for the series:
n1 = the series' total / (the least source deposit * particles per source), the crossings per
particle (params::noise::crossings_per_particle), or n1 * cv2, cv2 = Var L / (E L)^2 of the
particles' lifetimes from the band's room table (params::noise::lifetime_cv2). Energetic T20 and
T30 take one (k, kappa) for bands whose every face is Lambert with scattering 1 (the report's
reference, lambert_walls) and another for the rest; every other quantity one per method.

RULES (the code: crates/simpa-core/tests/common/noise_calibration.rs, "Round 3"):
R3-1. Per method, quantity and split: for each kappa in 0, 0.25, ..., 3, k = the largest over the
      calibration cells of the one-sided 95 % upper bound of the pooled ratio of the seeds'
      spread to sqrt(1 + kappa * n) * sd, rounded up to two significant digits; the kappa whose
      k overstates least (geometric mean over the cells of k over the pooled ratio) is chosen,
      the smaller on a tie. Energetic T20 and T30 outside Lambert bands keep k = 1 (M7's
      structure, as rounds 1 and 2 left them) and take the smallest kappa with every upper
      bound at most 1; if none does, F4.
R3-2. The variable per method: n1 or n1 * cv2, whichever makes the R3-1 fits of every quantity
      and split overstate least together (geometric mean over every cell, quantity and split);
      n1 on a tie.
R3-3. Every bound takes effective degrees of freedom: rows * 9 / (phi * deff), phi the dispersion
      of the rows' ratio^2 against the chi-square's 2/9 (at least 1), deff = 1 + (m - 1) * rho^2,
      rho^2 the mean squared correlation over the seeds of the values of two receivers of one
      band less its bias 1/9 (at least 0), m the mean rows per band.
R3-4. The splits as above; the Lambert split is calibrated on Lambert rows only, the other on the
      rest.
R3-5. The domain of a method, quantity and split: at least the fewest particles of a calibration
      cell with a row of it, and n at most the largest of any seed of its calibration rows. A
      value outside it is refused (a new reason, noise_uncalibrated), naming the particles that
      reach the domain, or how far the receiver radius must shrink (n grows as R^2).
R3-6. 1/sqrt(N), one-sided: on every pair of cells that differ only in N (rounds 1 and 2's six,
      and C3-R5/V3-R8, C3-R6/C-R5, C3-R9/V3-R9, C3-E5/V3-E5, C3-E6/C-E5, C3-E10/V3-E9),
      z = (the higher count's spread * sqrt(N) - the lower count's) / their joint standard error
      (on R3-3's degrees of freedom). A named count falls short only if the spread falls slower
      than 1/sqrt(N), so a quantity names no count when any pair gives z above 2. This replaces
      rule 4, which was two-sided; it is adopted after reading rounds 1 and 2's pairs, where it
      turns random T30 (-2.1) and energetic EDT (-2.5) to confirmed, and it is tested on the new
      pairs, which no rule has seen: the calibration pairs fix the flags before the validation;
      the validation pairs can only withdraw a count.
R3-7. Margins by rule 5b over round 3's calibration cells (per split for energetic T20 and T30).
R3-8. Validation, once, after every number above is committed in params::noise: on every V3 cell,
      method, quantity and split, over its rows inside the domain (and only if the cell's N is in
      it), the one-sided 95 % lower bound (R3-3) of the pooled ratio of the seeds' spread to
      k * sqrt(1 + kappa * n) * sd must be at most 1. Reported: the rows outside the domain.
      Coverage: every method, quantity and split should have at least two V3 cells with six rows
      inside the domain; a shortfall is reported, not made up.
R3-9. The named counts (rule 6) on every pair, with the shipped model, for every quantity;
      target 80 %, reported, not tuned.
R3-10. Say-NO partners in the suite on the committed receipt: the factors halved fail the
      validation; a quantity's observed spread raised above its prediction fails its check;
      kappa set to 0 where R3-1 chose more fails a cell that needs it (reported as found); a value
      outside the domain is refused.

THE STRUCTURE (fixed now; params::noise::roughness_deposits, Structure::Roughness): for energetic
T20 and T30 (and the curvature) in bands NOT uniform Lambert at a mean absorption up to 0.4 (round
3's other and Lambert splits together), each bin's mean deposit is read from the series itself:

the block takes d_j; other bins keep d_bar (M7's deposit). The resamples are M7's compound Poisson
draws with those deposits. The standard deviation is then k * sqrt(1 + kappa * n) * sd as in
round 3, n = n1 * cv2 (round 3's energetic variable). Every other quantity, energetic T20 and T30

      R3-3's effective degrees of freedom, a row taken only where every seed's roughness
      bootstrap gives a standard deviation.

      own scatter over the seeds (predicted_scatter_roughness); 1/sqrt(N) as R3-6 (the observed
      spreads do not depend on the structure).

      smallest multiple m of 2, 4, 8, 16, 32, 64 of the run's particles at which the model's own

      most 5 times (half the allowance, since a new run's own resamples refuse at a rate that
      scatters) and its calibrated standard deviation times the margin is within the limit; m * N

      A value refused for its standard deviation alone, its resamples within the allowance, names
      N * (margin * sd / limit)^2 as before.
