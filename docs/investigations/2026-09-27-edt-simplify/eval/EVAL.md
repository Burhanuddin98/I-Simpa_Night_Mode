# EDT-simplify evaluation: 3 candidates vs. upstream vs. the current band

Generated by `run_eval.py` + `run_report.py`, single process throughout, no solver run. JND used for wrong-silent/good/benefit-regression: 5% (ISO 3382-1 Annex A EDT figure; PRACTICE.md notes the one dedicated perceptual study found EDT's real JND closer to 18%, so this is a conservative, not lenient, bar).

## Interface and methods

All five methods are called through the same `analyse(bins, dt, t_arrival, meta) -> dict` interface on the same row:
- `cand_minimal`, `cand_leanband` (LeanBand), `cand_practice` -- unchanged, as submitted, called directly.
- `upstream` -- a thin wrapper around the validated f32 port of `Compute_TR_Param(0,10,...)` (`upstream-edt/uphunt_upstream_edt.py`). Upstream never refuses and never shows a range: `status`='ok' whenever the returned value is finite and positive (`edt_lo==edt_hi==edt`, a point value), 'refused' only on the NaN/non-positive degenerate case. This is upstream's real behaviour, not a wrapper artefact -- it is exactly what the READING.md/I7 finding means by "no range, ever".
- `band` -- a thin wrapper around `band_early.Setup(...).all_bands()` at its own real default tolerance (`LIMIT`, the 1/10-JND tau already shipped, not the containment-only `BIG_TAU=1e9` convention the old eval harness used for pure containment tables), fed the production tail inputs whenever a row's dataset provides them: on **real**, `tail_max`/per-bin `rel_eps` are computed by `band_early.production_inputs` from the same run-derived fields (`total_energy`/`trans_epsilon`/`particles_per_source`/killed-particle counts) GATE 2/4b use; on **t1**, the frozen golden case's own `tail_max`/`rel_eps`/`half_width` are used as built. On **z3**/**ism** (synthetic generators with no particle-count or trans_epsilon concept) there is no production statistic to feed, so `tail_max` is the generator's own known-negligible residual (matching `eval_run.py`'s own z3_job/ism_job convention for these sets, ~1e-9-1e-10 of the total energy) and `rel_eps=0` -- an honestly *near-exact* tail bound from a complete synthetic model, not an *absent* one; only real production runs ever force the degraded tail_max=0/rel_eps=0 path, and none of the four sets here exercise that exact path (the real set always has a computed, non-zero `production_inputs` tail).

**(d) is a reduced sample, not the full ~2,000 rows asked for.** Building one receiver's fine echogram (the specular-box image-source construction in `skeptic-gf3/ism.py`) measured 3-5 s each on this machine; a full ~2,000-row sample (535 registered receivers x up to 9 bands) would have cost on the order of half an hour of image-source generation alone, on top of the evaluation itself, which did not fit this task's single-process time budget. The sample actually used is every ~3rd of the 535 registered ISM receivers (stratified, not cherry-picked), each at 6 frequency bands x 2 steps (1 and 2 ms) -- **1920 rows**, regenerated with no solver. This is a real reduction in scope, stated plainly rather than padded.

## Cross-cutting observations

- **Upstream is silently wrong at a rate that varies by set but is never small**: 67.5% of t1, 87.3% of z3 (of the 204 cases with a finite truth), 17.2% of real, 41.4% of ism rows are `status=ok` (upstream never refuses) with |error| > 5%. Its p95 error reaches 232% on the z3 adversarial set and 49% on the ism sample.
- **The current band accepts almost nothing outside the one set it was tuned against**: 27.5% usable on t1 (its own golden corpus, with the golden case's own tail/eps inputs), 0% on z3, real and ism alike. Where it does accept (t1), it is never wrong (0 truth-outside-range) -- its certified property holds -- but at a measured cost of 60-500 ms/row against the candidates' <1 ms.
- **`cand_minimal` and `cand_leanband` track each other closely on point accuracy (near-identical median/p95 |err|%) but diverge sharply on wrong-silent**: leanband's tighter `ok`-acceptance gate (half-width <= JND *and* r2>=0.5, vs. minimal's r2>=0.70 alone) trades usable% for a consistently lower wrong-silent rate on every set (e.g. ism: 3/1920 vs. 301/1920).
- **`cand_practice` has the best point-accuracy and range-calibration (out-of-range) numbers on every real-ish set (real, ism)**, consistent with its Lundeby-style tail correction, but not the lowest wrong-silent rate -- its `ok` threshold (r2>=0.95) is looser on when a confidently-wrong *point value* still gets shown than leanband's half-width gate is, even though its *range* usually still contains the truth. Wrong-silent and out-of-range are measuring two different promises (the point value vs. the shown range); a method can do well on one and worse on the other.

## Finding: the candidates' 95%-style CI is well calibrated only when the direct sound has no spread (I2)

The "truth outside range" column above is far higher than a 95% interval should produce (5%) for `cand_minimal`/`cand_leanband` on t1 (35%) and real (12.7-13.2%). Splitting t1's 160 cases by their own `direct` setting (`impulse`, half_width=0, vs. `ball R=0.31`, half_width=R/c -- the receiver-ball spread of the direct-sound arrival, I2) isolates the mechanism exactly:

| method | impulse (h=0), out-of-range | ball R=0.31 (h=R/c), out-of-range |
|---|---|---|
| cand_minimal | 0/80 | 56/80 |
| cand_leanband | 0/80 | 56/80 |
| cand_practice | 0/80 | 16/80 |

`cand_minimal` and `cand_leanband` are never wrong when the direct sound is a true impulse (h=0) -- their OLS confidence interval is well-calibrated for the noise/fit scatter it is built to capture. Every one of their t1 misses is on the `ball R=0.31` half of the set, i.e. exactly the case both candidates' own DESIGN.md documents as undefended (I2: "no special weight needed -- causality already anchors it", treating the direct bin as a point when the receiver actually integrates it over a ball of finite radius). This is a **structural** bias (the fit window is offset by a fraction of the ball's transit time), not statistical noise, so an OLS standard-error interval -- which only measures scatter around whatever line was fit -- does not widen to cover it. `cand_practice`'s Lundeby-style iterative tail correction and split-half check catches part of this (0/40 on `ball,gap=0`) but not all of it (16/40 on `ball,gap>0`, still real). The real-data breakdown (every real receiver has a physical R=0.31 m ball, so this is not a t1-only artefact) confirms the same order of miss rate (12-13%) on both `cand_minimal` and `cand_leanband`, and 0/3600 for `cand_practice`.

## Crash-fixes log

None. All three candidates, the upstream wrapper and the band wrapper ran to completion on every row of every set with no exception.

## Caveat: the band numbers evaluate the current, unbatched algorithm only

They evaluate `band_early.Setup.all_bands()` directly, the actual, current, single-run code -- matching this task's own framing. `SPEC.md` Sec.8 additionally describes a not-yet-implemented seed-batch/noise-calibration layer (`noise_uncalibrated`, refusing every single-run EDT that is not part of a pooled seed batch); grepping `band_early.py` confirms that refusal reason does not exist in this file. So the band numbers here are for the algorithm as it actually runs today, and a fully wired production pipeline (per SPEC.md's own text) would refuse a single run's EDT even more often than measured here, not less.

## Runtime

The `ms/row` column above is mean wall time per `analyse()` call, single process, this machine. The three candidates and the upstream wrapper are all sub-millisecond to ~1 ms; the current band is 60-500 ms/row, two to three orders of magnitude slower -- not a bug, but the direct, documented cost of the certified search (`SPEC.md` Sec.7: up to `max_relax=6,000` relaxations per row, each `O(W)`, when the budget is not cut short by an early accept). This is a real, measured, operational cost of keeping the model-free guarantee, on top of its much lower acceptance rate.

## Note on candidates' own self-reported numbers

`cand_practice/DESIGN.md` cites a `stress_test.py` (300 randomized trials, 5.9% miss rate after its Lundeby-style fix) and a "bonus: 11,520 real receiver-band rows" check; `cand_leanband/DESIGN.md` cites a `smoke_test_leanband.py` "in the session's scratchpad". Neither script is present in the candidate's own directory in this repo, so those specific self-reported numbers could not be independently re-run here -- this evaluation's own numbers above (t1/z3/real/ism) are the ones actually re-verified in this session, and they corroborate the *direction* of both claims (cand_practice is the best-calibrated of the three on real data; cand_leanband refuses/degrades safely) without confirming their exact cited figures.
