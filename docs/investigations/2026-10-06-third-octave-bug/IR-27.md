## What "super detailed" can and cannot mean here

- **SPPS counts energy.** The echogram is Pa² per step per band. It has no phase and is not a pressure
  impulse response; a listenable IR is synthesised from it (the auralization plan, decision 67).
- **The frequency axis is the band set.** 27 bands is the densest preset. More bands cost time and map memory
  linearly (the data bound in `runSize.ts` is per band): 1/12 octaves would be about four times this run.
- **The bands are only as detailed as the data behind them.** The spectrogram shows plateaus three bands wide:
  the materials were defined per octave and the reband copies each octave's value to its three third-octave
  bands, and the sources' spectra likewise. The solver resolves 27 bands; the room's description resolves 9.
  Finer spectral detail needs third-octave material and source data, not a finer solver setting.
- **Time detail is 1 ms**, set by the time step; the time bin (patch 0001) is off here, so nothing is coarsened.
