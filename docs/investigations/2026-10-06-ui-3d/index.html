3D view, steps 1 and 2 of the depth recipe

The Elmia hall. "Now" is the app's view as it ships. "Shaded surfaces" changes only the surface material: faces lit by their orientation, mid greys on black. "See-through" adds the near walls back as glass, so the whole shell shows from outside. Edges and camera are the same in all three.

Drag to orbit, right-drag to pan, scroll to zoom. Keys 1, 2 and 3 switch.
