3D view: depth, step by step

"Now" is the app's view as it ships. "Shaded surfaces" changes only the surface material: faces lit by their orientation, mid greys on black. "See-through" adds the near walls back as glass, so the whole shell shows from outside. "Feature edges" draws only edges where surfaces meet at an angle, instead of every triangle. The camera is the same throughout.

Edges
Drag to orbit, right-drag to pan, scroll to zoom. Keys 1, 2 and 3 switch.
